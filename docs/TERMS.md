## Who this applies to
Fluxo is open-source software (MIT license) that you run yourself. This installation is operated by its owner
for **personal, non-commercial use only**: tracking the owner's own bank accounts. It is not offered to the public.

## What Fluxo does
- Reads account information (accounts, balances, transactions) from your bank through Enable Banking, **read-only**, only after you authorize it at your bank.
- Cannot make payments, move money or change anything at your bank.
- Stores the imported data on the operator's own computer to show spending, budgets and reports.

## Your consent and how to withdraw it
- Bank access is granted by you at your bank and lasts until it expires (typically up to 180 days) or you withdraw it.
- You can withdraw it at any time: Accounts → Disconnect in Fluxo, and/or in your bank's app (third-party / open-banking access).
- Disconnecting stops new imports. Already imported data stays until you delete it (Settings → Delete imported data / Delete my account).

## No financial advice, no guarantees
Fluxo shows information derived from your bank's data, including **estimates** (recurring payments, budgets). It is not financial advice.
Data can be incomplete or delayed (for example pending card payments, cash spending, or history the bank does not share).
